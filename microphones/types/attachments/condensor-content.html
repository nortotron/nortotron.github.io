1. Condensor

    Capacitor

    1. consists of two plates: one fixed, one moving
      1. As the front plate moves, the capacitance varies with the sound wave pressure
        1. Condensor mics require phantom power

          48v

          1. Generally, a wider frequency response than other types and are more sensitive
            1. But do watch high SPL’s- you may need to use a pad!
