dB of gain vs dBFS level

dBFS is a level: how big a signal is relative to digital full scale (0 dBFS = the largest sample value). dB of gain is a change: how much a fader adds or removes. A fader at 0 dB (unity) leaves the signal as it was: a −18 dBFS signal stays at −18 dBFS. Unity does not mean 0 dBFS.

Why this exercise uses identical signals

Every channel here is the same noise, perfectly correlated, so the peaks line up and add exactly. Real mixes are different: independent noise sources or different instruments do not peak together. For equal-level uncorrelated sources, average power rises about 3 dB per doubling, but that is a statistical average, not a guaranteed peak increase. Peaks can occasionally coincide, so real-world headroom planning still needs care.
